Decisions to approve

  1. Three scroll chapters, and only three: Proof, The Year and the way into Muharram, the places the brief marks for scrolling. Everything else on the site stays still and simple.
  2. Proof tells the story with drawings, not counters. A photo opens, a timeline draws from 1992 to 2018, ten lamps light for the ten nights, then the Hijri year turns as a ring with the six programs on it. It runs on phones too, with the drawing above the words.
  3. Placements on the year ring and in The Year are approximate: Shab-e-Qadr sits in the last third of Ramadan, Eid prayers on 1 Shawwal. [TODO: confirm which Eid prayers the institute holds]. The winter camp is placed in whichever Hijri month next January falls in, calculated automatically.
  4. --gold-text darkened from #8A6631 to #805D2B. The original failed AA (4.2:1) on the --paper-2 card color.
  5. Crimson is a fill, not a text color. On near-black it's only 1.9:1, so it's used for fills (with light text, 7:1), hairlines and card edges. Secondary text in Muharram mode uses --mourning-muted (#9C9893, 6.8:1).
  6. Added helper tokens within the existing roles: --mourning-2, --line-strong, --status-open, --status-full.
  7. Logo: images/logo.png isn't in the folder yet, so a simple line mark stands in for it.
