    Hero, in four layers

    The hall, the minbar and the haze sit inside the dome from the logo. The name stands in front of the minbar, and the crowd rises in front of the name, so the headline is inside the room rather than pasted on a picture. Each layer moves at its own rate as you scroll. The date and prayer times are live.

    For review

    1. The hero photoOne wide photo of a full Muharram night, taken from the back of the hall on a tripod at standing eye level, with the minbar and speaker centred and the crowd filling the bottom third. From the same tripod position, one frame of the empty hall before people arrive. A tall phone version of both helps the phone layout.
    2. What gets cut outThree layers: the empty hall as the background, the speaker and minbar cut out with a transparent background, and the two nearest rows of heads and shoulders cut out with a transparent background. The haze is made in code. Each goes in as a WebP.
    3. TypeArchivo replaces Cormorant Garamond and Manrope. Reem Kufi replaces Amiri for Arabic. Nothing is lighter than 400.
    4. ColourDeep teal is now the main surface. The light surface is a cool stone instead of ivory. The poster red is brighter (#8E1B1E) and only used for Muharram and "Live now".
    5. RemovedNumbered section labels, hairline rules, small capitals, middle dots, dashes in text and arrows on buttons. Cards are now three different things: timetable rows, photo tiles and video tiles.
